Spark: DragonWing

2022 · Solo project · 4 weeks · Unreal Engine 4

Platformer · Level Design · Lighting · Unreal Engine 4

A snowy night-time village level inspired by Spyro, built on the Spark project in Unreal Engine 4. The challenge: turn a small map into a long, rewarding route by building upward.

What I did

  • Used height and verticality to stretch a small footprint into a long level
  • Took the level from documentation through blockout to set dressing and lighting
  • Practised third-person movement and basic level Blueprints
