Spark: Skypiea Island

2022 · Solo project · 4 weeks · Unreal Engine 4

Platformer · Level Design · Unreal Engine 4

A floating-island platforming level inspired by Spyro, built on the Spark project in Unreal Engine 4.

What I did

  • Took the level through the full workflow: documentation, blockout, set dressing
  • Built the puzzles and gameplay objects in Blueprints, including wind tunnels and the final puzzle

Design highlights

  • Wind tunnels carry players to hidden parts of the map
  • Winding stairways lengthen the route without enlarging the map
  • Hidden boss fights reward players who explore
