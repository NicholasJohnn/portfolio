Retrovirus

2022 · Team project at CEA · 2 weeks · Unity

2D Puzzle Platformer · Level & Puzzle Design · Unity

Retrovirus is a 2D puzzle-platformer where you play a virus fighting its way to the cell's nucleus, mutating, dodging deadly obstacles and solving puzzles on the way.

My role: level and puzzle designer. Built in two weeks for the Rapid Prototyping course at the Centre for Entertainment Arts.

What I focused on

  • Building levels fast with Unity's 2D Tilemap editor
  • Designing puzzles that use every mechanic and every bit of available space
  • Shipping early versions quickly to leave time for playtesting and iteration
